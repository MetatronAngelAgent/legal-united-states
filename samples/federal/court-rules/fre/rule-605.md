# Text

The presiding judge may not testify as a witness at the trial. A party need not object to preserve the issue.

# History

(Pub. L. 93–595, § 1, Jan. 2, 1975, 88 Stat. 1934; Apr. 26, 2011, eff. Dec. 1, 2011.)
