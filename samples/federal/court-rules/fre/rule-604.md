# Text

An interpreter must be qualified and must give an oath or affirmation to make a true translation.

# History

(Pub. L. 93–595, § 1, Jan. 2, 1975, 88 Stat. 1934; Mar. 2, 1987, eff. Oct. 1, 1987; Apr. 26, 2011, eff. Dec. 1, 2011.)
