# Text

(a) The trustee must:

(1) file a list of the known names and addresses of entities entitled to payment from any remaining property of the estate that is paid into court under § 347(a); and

(2) include the amount due each entity.

(b) On the court’s website, the clerk must provide searchable access to information about funds deposited under § 347(a). The court may, for cause, limit access to that information in a specific case.
