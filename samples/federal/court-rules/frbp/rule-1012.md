# Text

(a) A debtor or a party in interest may contest a Chapter 15 petition for recognition of a foreign proceeding.

(b) Unless the court sets a different time, a response to the petition must be filed at least 7 days before the date set for a hearing on the petition.

(c) A corporation that responds to the petition must file a corporate-ownership statement containing the information described in Rule 7007.1. The corporation must do so with its first appearance, pleading, motion, or response, or other first request to the court.
