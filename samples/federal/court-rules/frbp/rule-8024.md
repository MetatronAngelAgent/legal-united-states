# Text

(a) After receiving the court’s opinion—or instructions if there is no opinion—the district or BAP clerk must:

(1) prepare and sign the judgment; and

(2) note it on the docket, which act constitutes entry of judgment.

(b) Immediately after a judgment is entered, the district or BAP clerk must:

(1) send notice of its entry, together with a copy of any opinion, to:• the parties to the appeal;
• the United States trustee; and
• the bankruptcy clerk; and

(2) note on the docket the date the notice was sent.

(c) On disposition of the appeal, the district or BAP clerk must return to the bankruptcy clerk any physical items sent as the record on appeal.
