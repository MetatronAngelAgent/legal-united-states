# Text

(a) The United States trustee may authorize a blanket bond in the United States’ favor—conditioned on the faithful performance of a trustee’s official duties—to cover:

(1) a person who qualifies as trustee in multiple cases; or

(2) multiple trustees who qualify in a different case.

(b) A party in interest may bring a proceeding in the United States’ name on a trustee’s bond for the use of the entity injured by the trustee’s breach of the condition.
