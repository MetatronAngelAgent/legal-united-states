# Text

(a) When a petition in an involuntary case is contested, the court must:

(1) rule on the issues presented at the earliest practicable time; and

(2) promptly issue an order for relief, dismiss the petition, or issue any other appropriate order.

(b) If the petition is not contested within the time allowed by Rule 1011, the court must issue the order for relief on the next day or as soon as practicable.
