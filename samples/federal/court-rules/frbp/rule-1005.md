# Text

(a) A petition’s caption must contain the name of the court, the title of the case, and the case number (if known). The title must include the following information about the debtor:

(1) name;

(2) employer-identification number;

(3) the last 4 digits of the social-security number or individual taxpayer-identification number;

(4) any other federal taxpayer-identification number; and

(5) all other names the debtor has used within 8 years before the petition was filed.

(b) A petition not filed by the debtor must include all names that the petitioner knows have been used by the debtor.
