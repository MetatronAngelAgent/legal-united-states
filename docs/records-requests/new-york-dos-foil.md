Under the New York Freedom of Information Law (Public Officers Law, Article 6, §§ 84–90), I request copies of the following records:

1. The complete current text of the Official Compilation of Codes, Rules and Regulations of the State of New York (all titles), in whatever electronic format the Department of State (Division of Administrative Rules) holds or receives it — for example XML, SGML, HTML, Word or database exports, including any files exchanged with the Department's official publisher.
